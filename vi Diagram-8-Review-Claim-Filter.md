# **§vi Diagram — 8-Review Claim Filter**

### *How a metabolic slogan becomes a graded claim, or stays a slogan.*

---

This diagram applies the Architecture of Truth 8-Review Trust Engine to the objects in this house. The source engine is `ArchitectureOfTruth` §vi. The filter question does not change.

## §vi.1 Reviews applied to this house

| Review | Goal in this house | Example on this object |
|--------|--------------------|------------------------|
| 1 Accord | Name the object | “We are discussing aerobic glycolysis in many tumors, not the slogan that cancer is diabetes.” |
| 2 Satisfaction | Name the form | “Warburg here means glucose to lactate with oxygen present. It does not mean ‘sugar causes cancer’ as a complete cause.” |
| 3 Accord + Satisfaction | Shared naming and form | Both readers hold the same object and the same sense of the words. |
| 4 Acceptance | Internal acknowledgment | The shared naming is accepted as the baseline for this chapter. |
| 5 Baseline | Stable anchor | Baseline is written in `§03` and in `literature/claims.md`. |
| Truth Filter | Fact, association, mechanism, metaphor, or not-supported | “Can this be measured, defined, or demonstrated?” |
| 6 Consideration | Weigh only graded statements | Population association is not treated as identity. |
| 7 Decision | Grade the claim | “Cancer is a type of diabetes” = metaphor. Shared insulin signaling = mechanism plus association. |
| 8 Action | Visible next check | Add or correct a row in `literature/claims.md`. Do not issue a protocol. |

## §vi.3 Distinctions that the filter must keep separate

**(a)** Planned intermittent hypoxia training ≠ obstructive sleep apnea ≠ tumor cyclic hypoxia

**(b)** Shared glucose/insulin pathways ≠ “cancer is a type of diabetes”

**(c)** Small human clove-and-glucose pilots ≠ a diabetes medicine ≠ a cancer medicine

**(d)** Zero hypoxia is not a single “neutral” that means “does nothing and causes no effect.” Dose, timing, disease, and whether the pulse is planned or uncontrolled all change the reading.
