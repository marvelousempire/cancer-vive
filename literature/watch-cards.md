## Cards

### W-008 Black seed / Nigella sativa

| Form | Lane | Band | Note |
|------|------|------|------|
| Culinary seed in food | food | Watch | Different object from oil |
| Black seed oil / thymoquinone-rich oil | adjunct / peri-op | Harm-flag this week | SPAQI-class hold before surgery (antiplatelet / glucose / CYP). Case report: peri-op serotonin syndrome with black seed oil. MSK: not shown to treat cancer in humans. |
| Isolated thymoquinone | investigational | Investigate | Small human trials exist (metabolic, oral leukoplakia, CRC adjunct). Not a breast-excision substitute. |

Grade: mechanism (cells/animals) + thin human adjunct. Human breast-cancer treatment: not-supported.

### W-009 Black walnut

| Form | Lane | Band | Note |
|------|------|------|------|
| Culinary walnut meat | food | Watch | Not the hull |
| Black-walnut hull extract / “parasite cleanse” | ungraded kit | Harm-flag | Zero human RCTs for any indication. Juglone toxicity in animals. ACS: hulls not shown to remove intestinal parasites. Tree-nut allergy risk. |

### W-010 Wormwood (Artemisia absinthium)

| Form | Lane | Band | Note |
|------|------|------|------|
| Culinary / bitters trace | food | Watch | Not the oil |
| Herb / tea short course | traditional | Stay-away this week | Thujone. Not for pregnancy, seizure disorder, ragweed allergy. |
| Concentrated oil or high-dose cleanse | toxicity | Harm-flag | Human hepatotoxicity case reports, including acute liver failure. EMA-class thujone daily caps exist because the object is not inert. |

Closed: this is not artemisinin from sweet wormwood (A. annua).

### W-011 Shilajit resin

| Form | Lane | Band | Note |
|------|------|------|------|
| Purified, batch-tested resin | mineral pitch | Watch / disclose | Product is not standardized. ConsumerLab: fulvic acid varied ~32,000% across brands. |
| Raw / untested resin | contamination | Harm-flag | Unprocessed samples can carry lead, arsenic, mercury, cadmium, thallium. MSK-class: not a cancer treatment; discuss in active care. |

### W-012 Humic / fulvic acid

Different object from whole shilajit resin (W-011). Isolated soil extracts are not a breast-cancer treatment. Quality and metal load inherit the same contamination problem unless a COA exists. Band: Watch + disclose; do not start a new bottle this week without the surgical team.

### W-018 Hydrogen peroxide 3%

Named object: drugstore 3% H2O2. Different objects: 35% industrial / “food grade,” ingested “oxygen therapy,” IV infusion, clinician-directed wound irrigant.

| Form | Lane | Band | Note |
|------|------|------|------|
| 3% topical on intact skin / surface disinfectant | household | Watch | Label antiseptic. Not a tumor treatment. |
| 3% on a surgical wound | wound care | Stay-away unless the surgeon owns it | Can injure fibroblasts; modern wound practice often prefers not to use it on healing tissue. |
| Swallowed 3% as “oxygen therapy” | ingestion | Harm-flag | GI irritation and chemical injury. Not shown to treat cancer. |
| 35% “food grade” or any internal / IV use | oxidative slogan | Harm-flag | FDA: no proven medical value internally; can cause burns, gas embolism, death. NCI: no support for rubbing it on skin as a cancer cure. |

Slogan closed: cancer as hypoxia that a brown bottle will oxygenate. That is metaphor (see §04 / §05). Warburg / HIF work does not hatch this bottle.

This house does not file a recipe for internal use.

## This-week heat (detriment first)

Harm-flag / do not start new: wormwood oil or cleanse kit; black-walnut hull kit; new black-seed oil; high-dose curcumin; untested shilajit; concentrated cayenne capsules; swallowed or infused hydrogen peroxide; 35% “food grade” peroxide.

Disclose if already in the house: any of the above, plus magnesium, zinc, culinary spices, and a 3% brown bottle used only as labeled surface antiseptic.

Checkable later (not G1): confirm magnesium and zinc on labs if the team will draw them; COA for any resin already purchased; keep food spices as food.

## How a card moves

A card only leaves Harm-flag or Stay-away when a human measurement in this window exists (lab, pathology, or a clinician-owned plan). Metaphor and cell papers do not hatch G1.
