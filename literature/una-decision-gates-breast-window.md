# UNA Decision Gates — breast window

Framework: Semantic Node / Gift Egg. One process, one egg. Approval is per egg.
A hospital visit may sit several eggs on one table. UNA does not fuse the yolks.

Verb: `ApproveOneGateAtATime`
Closed: do not treat surgery + radiation + chemotherapy as one object.
Closed: this file is not a refusal script and not a finding of bad faith.
Closed: this house does not treat, prescribe, or delay the named operation.

```
G1 SURGERY  ->  G2 PATHOLOGY  ->  G3 RADIATION?  ->  G4 SYSTEMIC?  ->  G5 GENETICS?
   this week      after tissue      local             whole body        later
                  is out            neighborhood      only if G2 says
```

Rule: no next egg hatches until the current egg has a receipt.
Receipt = written name of the step + why + what the tissue or scan showed + what happens if they wait to decide the NEXT egg.

## Gate cards

### G1 Surgery (this week)
- Shell: remove the biopsied 1 cm mass.
- Yolk receipts: operation name; what pathology will print; disclose list for anesthesia (vape, wine, prior low iron/magnesium, every pill / tea / oil / extract already in the house).
- Closed: spice or homeopathy as a substitute.

### G2 Pathology (the switch)
- Shell: tissue report.
- Yolk receipts: DCIS vs invasive; grade; margins; nodes; ER / PR / HER2.
- Until this yolk is filled, chemo is not treated as already true.

### G3 Radiation (local)
- Shell: energy aimed at the breast neighborhood after the lump is out, usually when the breast is kept.
- Not chemotherapy.
- At 41, radiation-omission evidence built for older low-risk patients does not automatically apply.
- Receipt questions: why this breast; what local-recurrence number they are using; is this a separate consent from G1; can it wait on G2.

### G4 Systemic (chemo / hormone / none)
- Default: not hatched.
- A 1 cm finding does not mean chemotherapy is required.
- Opens only if G2 says the rest of the body needs a conversation.

### G5 Genetics
- Later. Paternal aunt + paternal grandmother + age 41.
- Not the operating room.
