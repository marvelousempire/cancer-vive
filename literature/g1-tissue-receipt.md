## What a G2 tissue report is

Not present. Not invented. Listed only so G1 is not asked to hold G2 yolks.

CAP resection protocol for an excision less than total mastectomy, or a mastectomy, is the paper that prints after G1 if the mass is removed. Core fields on that paper, when the specimen exists, include:

- Procedure name and laterality
- Histologic type
- Invasive vs DCIS
- Grade
- Size on the excision specimen
- Margin status (ink on tumor is the CAP positive mark)
- Nodes if they were in the specimen
- ER / PR / HER2 on the CAP biomarker template

Those lines are empty here. A 1 cm biopsy size is not the excision size. One series of 922 cases measured mean 7.5 mm on biopsy and 14.4 mm on excision; 82% were larger on excision.
