# Spike proteins: what you need to know

This lesson is about the spike on SARS-CoV-2, the virus that causes COVID-19.

## What is spike?

Spike is a protein on the virus surface. It helps the virus enter a cell. Spike is not the whole virus. A lab test of spike is not the same as a test of infection.

[Read the spike structure study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32075877/)

## Why do vaccines use it?

COVID shots help immune cells learn to spot spike. An mRNA shot has instructions to make a spike target. The mRNA is broken down by the body. A protein shot has the target itself. These shots do not contain a live COVID virus.

[Read how COVID-19 vaccines work](https://www.cdc.gov/covid/vaccines/how-they-work.html)

## Do COVID-19 vaccines cause cancer?

NCI reports no evidence that COVID-19 shots cause cancer, make it return, or make it grow. A cell test alone does not prove those claims in people. NCI is the U.S. National Cancer Institute.

[Read the NCI cancer guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/coronavirus/covid-19-vaccines-people-with-cancer)

## What did the infection study show?

A 2025 study used mice with sleeping breast-cancer cells in the lungs. Cell growth was seen after flu or COVID infection. Human records linked COVID with cancer deaths and lung spread of breast cancer. Those links do not prove cause. The study did not test a shot or spike alone.

[Read the infection and cancer study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40739350/)

## Can one alarming paper settle it?

A 2021 paper about spike and DNA repair was retracted in 2022. The paper was withdrawn by the journal. DNA is the cell's gene code. The paper is not sound proof that COVID shots cause cancer.

[Read the journal retraction](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4915/14/5/1011)

## What is useful during cancer care?

Ask the team about infection risk and shot timing. A shot can swell lymph nodes, small glands that help fight germs. The swelling can affect some scans. Tell the scan team when and where you had the shot. New or lasting swelling needs a check.

[Read the NCI scan guidance](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/coronavirus/covid-19-vaccines-people-with-cancer)

## Are there vaccine risks?

Sore arms and fever can occur. Rare serious effects, including heart inflammation, are tracked. Your risks and care plan matter. Ask for current advice for your age and health, not a fixed schedule from this page.

[Read the vaccine safety guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccine-safety/vaccines/covid-19.html)
