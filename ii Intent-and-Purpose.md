# **§ii Intent & Purpose**

---

### *The purpose of this house is simple: to keep a checkable record of what was asked, what was named, what was graded, and what sources support each claim.*

### *The first research object is the metabolic overlap of type 2 diabetes and some cancers. The session that opened this house named cloves and glucose handling, the slogan “cancer is a type of diabetes,” Warburg mechanisms, pseudohypoxia, and intermittent hypoxia training.*

### *The intent is to separate four kinds of statement:*

**(a)** a measurement that can be checked

**(b)** a population association

**(c)** a laboratory or pathway mechanism

**(d)** a slogan or metaphor that is useful in speech and unsafe as a diagnosis

### *Nothing here is written to treat, prescribe, or replace a clinician. Chapters use reviews, not steps. Examples illustrate. They do not tell the reader what to take, cut, breathe, or stop.*

### *This handbook is a Handbook Framework Contribution Type within the Contribution Network — an In Good Faith With Clean Hands Association.*

### *Love is an action.:* ~ The Brown-Santa ™

### *In this house, that means we explain why a claim sits in its grade. We keep the tone calm. We give the smallest checkable source path so the reader can verify.*
