## Evidence state at filing

### Ivermectin

- NCI identifies ivermectin as an antiparasitic drug.
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05318469 is studying ivermectin with immune-checkpoint therapy in metastatic triple-negative breast cancer.
- ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07487805 is studying ivermectin with immune-checkpoint inhibition in adult solid tumors.
- Cancer Vive position: investigational human oncology evidence; established breast-cancer treatment claim remains not-supported.

### Hydroxychloroquine

- NCI lists current oncology trials using hydroxychloroquine, including breast-cancer research.
- FDA labeling includes clinically important safety issues including QT prolongation and retinal toxicity.
- Cancer Vive position: investigational human oncology evidence; established breast-cancer treatment claim remains not-supported.

## Safety boundary
