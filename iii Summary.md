# **§iii Summary**

### *Cancer Vive is a research ledger, not a clinic. It holds one research object: the metabolic overlap between type 2 diabetes and some cancers, plus the related claims that appeared in the opening session.*

### *The opening session named five objects in order:*

**(a)** cloves as a glucose-handling adjunct

**(b)** the slogan that cancer is a type of diabetes when looked at scientifically

**(c)** Warburg effect mechanisms (aerobic glycolysis)

**(d)** pseudohypoxia mechanisms (HIF-1α stable when oxygen is present)

**(e)** intermittent hypoxia training, and how it differs from sleep apnea and from tumor cyclic hypoxia

### *The Truth Filter used here is the same filter as Architecture of Truth: can the statement be measured, defined, or demonstrated? If yes, it may enter as fact, association, or mechanism. If no, it stays opinion or metaphor.*

### *The standing grades are:*

| Grade | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| shown | Human measurement that can be checked |
| association | Linked in populations; cause not settled |
| mechanism | Lab / animal / pathway evidence |
| metaphor | Useful slogan that is not a diagnosis |
| not-supported | Does not survive the Truth Filter |

### *The standing distinctions that must not collapse:*

**(a)** Planned intermittent hypoxia training is not obstructive sleep apnea.

**(b)** Tumor cyclic hypoxia is not a training protocol.

**(c)** Zero hypoxia is not a simple “neutral” that does nothing. Context, dose, timing, and disease state change the meaning.

**(d)** Shared glucose and insulin pathways do not make cancer a type of diabetes.
