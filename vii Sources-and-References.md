## D. What does not count as a source here

**(a)** A social post that restates the slogan without a measurement

**(b)** A protocol blog that tells the reader what to take or breathe

**(c)** An ungraded model answer that treats metaphor as diagnosis

## E. How a new source enters

1. Name the claim in one sentence.
2. Assign a grade (`shown` / `association` / `mechanism` / `metaphor` / `not-supported`).
3. Add the bibliographic row to `literature/sources.md`.
4. Point the claim row at that source id.
5. If the source is a dictionary anchor, also add it to section A or C above.
