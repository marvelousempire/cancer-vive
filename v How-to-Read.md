## Purpose of this schema

This house copies the Architecture of Truth numbering and review bands so literature written here can sit next to other Concise Perspective handbooks without a second schema.

Schema source: `marvelousempire/ArchitectureOfTruth` — `v How to Read This Handbook.md`.

## Numbering

### Front matter (Roman)

`i`, `ii`, `iii`, `iv`, `v`, `vi`, `vii`

Front matter can grow. Chapter codes do not move when a new Roman page is added.

### Chapters (Arabic section codes)

Format: `§[chapter number]` starting at `§01`.

Example: `§04 Warburg Effect Mechanisms`

### Sections and subsections

- Section: `§[file].[section]` — example `§04.3`
- Subsection: `§[file].[section](letter)` — example `§04.3(a)`
- Nested lists: `(a)` then `(i)` then `(A)`

File numbers (01, 02, 03) are disk order. Section codes (`§01`, `§04`) are the stable reference.

## Chapter bands

Every chapter uses the same A–E bands after the opening question.

| Band | Job |
|------|-----|
| A. Honorable Roles | Who is reading this chapter and what they are invited to hold |
| B. Key Definitions | Dictionary or field anchors. Keep aligned with `vii` and `literature/sources.md` |
| C. Chapter Frame | How this chapter fits the house. How common speech differs from the named object |
| D. Common Phrases | Everyday slogans and variants that surface the pattern |
| E. Core Teaching | The body. Mechanisms, distinctions, and graded claims live here |

Closing practice section: **Honorable Mention**. Habits and “If This, Then That” tools. Chapter-themed examples only.

## Language safety

This house does not provide medical, legal, or personal advice.

Safe forms used here:

- “Example observation” instead of “You should take”
- “See the grade and the source” instead of “Do this protocol”
- “Illustrates” instead of “Prescribes”

Reviews, not steps. The Trust Engine is eight reviews. This house does not install a self-help program.

Accessible Language Tone: calm, front-desk, 9-year-old standard. A technical term is named, then said in plain words before it is used again.

## Claim grades

Every claim in `literature/claims.md` and in chapter body text carries one grade.

| Grade | Meaning | May enter Review 6–8? |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| shown | Human measurement that can be checked | Yes, as fact |
| association | Linked in populations; cause not settled | Yes, labeled as association |
| mechanism | Lab / animal / pathway evidence | Yes, labeled as mechanism |
| metaphor | Useful slogan that is not a diagnosis | No. Stays in opinion lane |
| not-supported | Does not survive the Truth Filter | No |

## Truth Filter question

*Can this statement be measured, defined, or demonstrated?*

If yes, name the grade and the source.
If no, keep it as opinion or metaphor and do not build later reviews on it.
